Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to clean out the house first?

No. Leave anything you don't want to keep: furniture, boxes, trash, the stuff in the attic. Take what matters to you and I handle the rest.

Do I need to make any repairs?

No. I buy the house as-is. Mold, termites, fire or water damage, foundation issues, a roof that leaks: you don't fix anything.

Are there fees or commissions?

No realtor, so no commission, and no fees. The offer I make is the offer we talk about.

Will there be showings or inspections?

No showings and no inspections. You don't have strangers walking through the house, and nobody backs out after an inspection report.

How fast can we close?

On your timeline. We can make it happen fast if you need the money now, or wait if you need time to sort things out.

Is the offer free?

Yes. The cash offer is free and you're under no obligation to accept it.

Where do you buy?

New Jersey, mostly North Jersey. Send me the town and a few details, and I'll let you know.
