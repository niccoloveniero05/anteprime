Privacy and cookies

This page explains how the personal data of visitors to the site, and of those who send a request through the contact form, are processed under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Data controller

The data controller is Mattia Malpeli. Any request concerning personal data can be sent to the contact details shown on the Contact page.

Data collected

The form collects full name, company, email, phone (optional), type of transaction and the brief description entered. Please do not enter sensitive data or confidential information in the form.

While the site is being browsed, the hosting provider’s systems record technical data, such as IP address and time of the visit, needed for the site to work securely.

Purposes and legal basis

Form data are used only to reply to the request and, where appropriate, to arrange a meeting, as a pre-contractual step requested by the data subject (art. 6.1.b GDPR). Technical data are processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in the operation and security of the site (art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Retention

Request data are kept for as long as needed to handle them and in any case for no more than 24 months, unless a professional relationship begins.

Recipients

Data are neither sold nor disclosed. They may be processed by technical providers, for hosting the site and handling the form, acting as data processors.

Rights

At any time it is possible to request access to one’s data, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection to processing and portability, by writing to the data controller. It is also possible to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Cookies

The site does not use profiling cookies or third-party analytics or advertising tools. Fonts and scripts are loaded from the site itself, with no connections to external services: for this reason no consent banner is shown.

Should tools requiring consent be added in future, consent will be requested before they are activated.
