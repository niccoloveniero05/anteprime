Profile

Not a generalist adviser. Someone who takes part in the transaction.

Mattia Malpeli works alongside entrepreneurs, companies and investors in situations that a written opinion cannot resolve: crises, reorganisations, extraordinary transactions, acquisitions.

Each case is assessed on its own terms, with no ready-made solutions. When the mandate requires it, the assessment is followed by direct involvement: coordinating professionals, directing the operational phases, taking on formally conferred corporate or management roles.

Experience

Different situations, no standard answers.

Companies of different sizes, complexity and objectives. In some cases the work was aimed at restructuring and continuing the business, in others at divestment or definitive closure.

The choice of path comes after the analysis, never before.

Method

II
