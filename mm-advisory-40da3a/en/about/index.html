The profile

Assess with clarity, step in when needed.

Mattia Malpeli works on corporate restructuring, extraordinary corporate transactions and private investments, independently, with entrepreneurs, companies and investors facing delicate transitions who need someone who does not stop at the analysis.

Not a generalist adviser, but someone who looks at the situation as a whole, identifies the path that can truly be taken and, when the mandate requires it, takes part directly in carrying it out.

20+

business situations in the last 24 months

Experience

Different situations, no standard answers.

Companies of different sizes, complexity and objectives. In some cases the work was aimed at restructuring and continuing the business, in others at divestment or definitive closure.

The choice of path comes after the analysis, never before.

Philosophy

Sometimes the right choice is to carry on. Sometimes it is to close in good order.

There are no solutions that fit everyone. Every situation has its own history, constraints and real options: the work is to see them clearly and to decide with lucidity, even when the decision is a difficult one.

Way of working

From the first meeting to execution.

  1. 01

    Confidential meeting

    A first conversation, direct and confidential, to understand the situation and whether there are grounds to work together.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Analysis of the corporate, financial and operational position. No predefined solution: the real alternatives are weighed, including divestment or closure.

  3. 03

    Plan and team

    Definition of the path and of the professionals required: legal, accounting, corporate and management, coordinated under one direction.

  4. 04

    Execution

    Operational coordination of the plan and, when formally conferred, direct appointment to corporate or management roles.
