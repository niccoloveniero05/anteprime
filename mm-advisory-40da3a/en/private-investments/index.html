Private Investments & Acquisitions

Private investments and acquisitions

Alongside advisory and management work, Mattia Malpeli assesses direct investments with own capital.

  1. Assessment of investments with own capital.
  2. Acquisition of equity stakes.
  3. Purchase of companies, business units and individual assets.
  4. Assessment of direct financing, within the limits allowed by applicable law.
  5. Identification of business opportunities with potential for recovery, development or value creation.
Note

Investments are made exclusively with own capital. No funds are raised from third parties and no investment advice is provided. Any direct financing is assessed within the limits allowed by applicable law.

Size

No predefined size range.

Each transaction is assessed on its complexity and on the concrete opportunities it offers, not on the size of the company.
