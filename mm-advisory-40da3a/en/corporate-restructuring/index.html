Corporate Restructuring & Turnaround

Restructuring and turnaround

When a business still has value to protect, the aim is to rebuild the conditions for it to continue.

  1. Corporate restructuring and reorganisation.
  2. Assessment of recovery and business continuity options.
  3. Planning and coordination of operational measures.
  4. Reorganisation of corporate and management structures.
B

Crisis Management & Corporate Exit

Crisis management and exit

When a crisis is under way, it takes timely decisions, order and clear direction across all the professionals involved.

  1. Strategic management of corporate crises.
  2. Operational support in pre-insolvency phases.
  3. Assessment of divestment or definitive closure options.
  4. Coordination of legal, accounting and corporate professionals.

Approach

No outcome taken for granted.

Not every situation leads to continuity, and not every situation should. In some cases the way forward is recovery, in others the sale of a business unit or the definitive closure of the company. The assessment is there to establish which path is truly viable, and to manage it in good order.
